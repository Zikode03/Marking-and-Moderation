PROSPERA ASSESS — STAFF QUICK START

1. SIGN IN
Select school or institution, public or private, and your academic pathway. Use the staff account issued by your organisation. Ask its administrator if you need access.

2. FIND YOUR WORK
School staff work with assigned Grades 8–12 subjects. Institution staff work with assigned faculties and modules.

3. UPLOAD EXISTING ASSESSMENTS
Choose the academic allocation and upload the existing question paper, approved memorandum and candidate scripts. This system does not create assessments.

4. MARK AND REVIEW
AI marks require a connected, trained and validated local model. Check model status in the workspace. Review scripts against the memo, inspect flags and record reasons for changes. Flags invite academic review; they do not prove an error.

5. MODERATE AND APPROVE
Use your assigned role to moderate and approve results. Keep final academic decisions with authorised staff.

6. TRAIN RESPONSIBLY
Only verified examples should enter the training dataset. Evaluate handwriting, language and marking performance before using a model for live work.
